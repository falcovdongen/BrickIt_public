Data Deletion Request

App: BrickIt  |  Last updated: November 4, 2026

To have your BrickIt account and personal data permanently deleted, follow the steps below. All data will be removed within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

When you request deletion, we permanently remove:

  • Your account (email address and the name linked to your Google or Apple sign-in)
  • Your entire LEGO® collection: sets, parts and minifigures, including quantities, conditions and favorites
  • Your Rebrick list and any other saved data
  • Any other personal data stored on our servers

Data stored only on your device, such as app preferences, is removed when you uninstall BrickIt.

Subscription records held by the Google Play Store, the Apple App Store or RevenueCat, and advertising data held by Google AdMob, are governed by their own retention policies and are not within our control to delete. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How to request deletion

  • Send an email to falcovdongen@gmail.com with the subject line: BrickIt Data Deletion Request
  • Include the email address associated with your BrickIt account so we can locate your data.
  • We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Questions

If you have any questions about the deletion process, contact us at falcovdongen@gmail.com.
