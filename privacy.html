Privacy Policy

App: BrickIt  |  Last updated: November 4, 2026

Summary: BrickIt stores your LEGO® collection securely in the cloud so you can access it across devices. We show ads to keep the app free and offer an optional premium subscription. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who We Are

BrickIt ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Falco van Dongen. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at falcovdongen@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account Data

When you sign in, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (when using Google or Apple sign-in; with Apple you can choose to hide your email address)

This data is used solely to identify your account and sync your collection across devices.

Collection Data

The core function of the app is tracking your LEGO collection. We store:

  • Sets, parts and minifigures you add to your collection, including quantities, condition (new or used) and favorites
  • Sets you add to your Rebrick list

Camera

If you grant camera permission, BrickIt uses the camera to scan set numbers and QR codes. Scanning happens entirely on your device. Camera images are not stored and are never uploaded to our servers. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings.

Device and Advertising Data

To serve ads, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or the iOS Identifier for Advertisers if you allow tracking)
  • IP address
  • Device type, OS version, and app activity

This data is used to show you relevant advertisements. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android under Privacy > Ads, on iOS under Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Purchase Data

If you subscribe to BrickIt Premium, our payments partner RevenueCat processes your purchase. We receive your subscription status (active, expired, etc.) but do not store payment details such as card numbers. All payments are handled by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.

Data Stored on Your Device

Some preferences, such as whether you have completed the app introduction, are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and synchronise your LEGO collection across devices
  • To show which sets you can build from the parts you own (Rebrick)
  • To show the estimated value of your collection
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To display advertisements that help fund the free version of the app
  • To process and verify your premium subscription

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers listed in Section 4, to the extent necessary to operate the app
  • If required by law, for example in response to a court order or legal obligation
  • To protect rights or safety, where we believe disclosure is necessary to prevent harm

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and collection data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will permanently delete your personal data and collection within 30 days. See our data deletion page for how to request this.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing for direct marketing purposes
  • Withdraw consent — change your ad consent choice at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at falcovdongen@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

BrickIt is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS) and secure cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
